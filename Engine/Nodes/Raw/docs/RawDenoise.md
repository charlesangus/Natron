# RawDenoise

Tolerance: none recorded.

Port of darktable's raw denoise module (`src/iop/rawdenoise.c`, darktable `release-5.6.2`), and
of the C path of `dwt_denoise()` (`src/common/dwt.c`). The kernels are
`RawKernels::rawDenoiseBayer()` and `rawDenoiseXTrans()` in `RawDenoiseKernel.cpp`.

- Raw in, raw out, at the prepared stage. Any other stage is a persistent error.
- Each colour of the mosaic (the four of the Bayer 2x2 block, or the three X-Trans colours filled
  in at every photosite from their neighbours) is square-rooted to even out its noise. It is then
  decomposed into five à trous wavelet bands whose details are soft thresholded, and squared back.
- **Threshold**: the overall strength, 0.01 by default. 0 is an identity.
- **All Channels / Red / Green / Blue Strengths**: darktable's per-band curves, sampled at its
  five bands from coarsest to finest, 0.5 neutral. A band's threshold is darktable's detail
  constant x (16 x all^2 x channel^2)^2 x Threshold, so 0 in a band leaves it alone. darktable's
  curves are Catmull-Rom splines through five knots whose positions its interface never moves, so
  the five values are the knots themselves.
- Data without a CFA passes through unchanged with a persistent warning. darktable's module only
  runs on mosaics.
- No method choice and no ISO input: darktable's raw denoise has one method, the wavelets, and
  does not read the ISO or a noise profile.
- **No noise profiles.** At `release-5.6.2`, `rawdenoise.c` does not use
  `data/noiseprofiles.json`. Only the profiled denoise module does, which is not ported here, so
  no profile data ships.
- **Tiling.** A render reads its window grown by what the bands reach: 64 photosites on a Bayer
  mosaic (31 samples of a colour sampled every other photosite) and 66 on an X-Trans one. The
  X-Trans halo is twice 31 + 2 because darktable never fills green in the image's last column, so
  there green is denoised over the red result left in its buffer. Windows are aligned to the CFA
  period from the source's top-left photosite. A tile is bit for bit the full frame.
- darktable splits the X-Trans extraction into one chunk of rows per thread and repairs the rows
  the chunks share. This port extracts in one pass, the result that repair reproduces.
- It renders at full resolution only.

Tests: `Tests/Native/NativeRawDenoise_Test.cpp`, on synthetic Bayer and X-Trans mosaics. Gaussian
noise of 0.01 on a smooth mosaic with a 0.4 step loses more than 3 dB (about 5 measured), and the
photosites next to the step stay within 0.06 of it (0.04 measured). Tiles match the full frame,
per-channel strengths act on their colour only, and linear data passes through with a warning.
