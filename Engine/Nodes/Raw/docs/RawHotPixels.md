# RawHotPixels

Tolerance: none recorded.

Port of darktable's hot pixels module (`src/iop/hotpixels.c`, darktable `release-5.6.2`), C path
only. It is a Passes op: one gather pass with a 2-photosite halo, 1 without a CFA.

- Raw in, raw out, at the prepared stage. Any other stage is a persistent error.
- **Threshold**: only a photosite above it can be hot, 1 being the white level.
- **Strength**: a photosite is hot when strength / 2 times its value is above each of its
  neighbours of the same colour. It then becomes the largest of them. 0 is an identity, as it
  disables darktable's module.
- **Detect By 3 Neighbours**: darktable's "permissive" mode. Three of the four neighbours are
  enough, which also catches two hot pixels side by side.
- The neighbours, as in darktable:
  - On a Bayer mosaic, the photosites two away left, right, up and down.
  - On an X-Trans mosaic, the first four of the same colour in darktable's search order around
    the photosite.
  - Without a CFA, the adjacent pixels, per channel. darktable has this only for monochrome
    sensors, where it tests the first channel and writes all of them.
- Photosites within 2 of the image's edge, 1 without a CFA, are left as they are, as in
  darktable. Edges are the source's, never a tile's, so tiles match the full frame.
- darktable's "mark fixed pixels" is a darkroom display aid and is not ported, nor is the count
  of fixed pixels it shows.
- It renders at full resolution only.

Tests: `Tests/Native/NativeRawHotPixels_Test.cpp`, on synthetic Bayer, X-Trans and linear data.
Hot pixels injected into a dim smooth mosaic become the largest of their neighbours, and nothing
else changes. A clean mosaic comes back bit for bit, and tiles match the full frame.
