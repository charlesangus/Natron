glass.json is converted by tools/optics/convert_glass.py from the .mtrl files of
OpticsDatabase (https://github.com/amegahed/OpticsDatabase, commit
1b007269eda014b9e4d23670d5d5d7dd2dfe6de5), whose glass data comes from the
refractiveindex.info database by Mikhail Polyanskiy (CC0 1.0).

lenses.zmx.gz is converted by tools/optics/convert_lenses.py from the
Optics/Photography .zmx files of the same OpticsDatabase commit. Each lens keeps
its original path (#SOURCE) and NOTE lines. OpticsDatabase credits these
contributors for its content:
 - Bill Claff - https://photonstophotos.net
 - Mikhail Polyanskiy - https://refractiveindex.info
 - Michael Hayford - https://github.com/mjhoptics
 - Katsumoto (Kats) Ikeda - https://www.pencilofrays.com
