# 2026-10-27 — Cycles and MoonRay as USD renderers

USDRender and the 3D viewer gain a selectable Hydra render delegate (M248 - Render Delegate Selection), with Storm the default and the viewer's choice independent of USDRender's. Cycles and MoonRay are built against the pinned USD, CPU only, and bundled in the dev image and AppImage (M249 - Bundle Cycles Delegate, M250 - Bundle MoonRay Delegate); GPU devices are a later milestone. Settings are curated knobs plus a generated fallback (M253 - Delegate Render Settings), the viewer refines progressively with restart on edit up to a Max Samples cap (M254 - Progressive Viewer Render). Hydra has no deep AOV, so the deep transport is not chosen up front: M255 - Delegate Output Spikes settles it, and the AOV-selection mechanism, before M256 - Delegate AOV Selection, M257 - Cycles Deep Output and M258 - MoonRay Deep Output are elaborated. A deep EXR side file is not an accepted transport unless the user approves it after the spike. Rationale: USD substrate decision (2026-09-05-adopt-typed-edges-and-usd-substrate) and the no-side-channel principle.
