# Follow overwrites keys; blending and reduction are separate actions

Follow forward, backward and range in Roto, RotoPaint and SplineWarp start from the shape at the start frame and write a key on every followed frame, replacing existing keys there. Keys outside the followed frames are untouched, and each follow is one undo step. This supersedes the M219 - Curves Follow Vectors decisions of 2026-10-25 (existing keys are anchors bridged by a crossfade) and 2026-11-07 (track back from the nearest later key). The user's reason: once a follow fills every frame, anchors make re-following a no-op. Blending between existing keys with the vectors becomes its own "Blend Between Keys" action. Keyframe reduction leaves follow and becomes a standalone "Reduce Keyframes" post-process, usable on any shape at any time. This holds until a separate follow animation layer (requested as a follow-up milestone) lets follow results and hand keys coexist. Decided by the user on 2026-11-16, during F8 - Motion Vectors UAT.
