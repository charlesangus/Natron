# MoonRay deep output travels over Arras

MoonRay stays out of process for every render, deep included. Deep output reaches Natron through a new banded DeepFrame Arras message, sent after the FINISHED progressive frame and before `stopFrame`. It is serialised from `RenderContext::getDeepBuffer()` in row bands, so no message nears the 4 GiB buffer limit or the chunking copy, and it is received by hdMoonray into a `HydraDeepBuffer` returned from `HdRenderBuffer::GetResource()` (the transport M286 - Utility Renderer Deep Output defines). Deep is final-frame only. The rejected options were: (a) in-process rendering for deep (loses crash and memory isolation and concurrent renders; one global RenderDriver; process-wide TBB cap); (e) a deep EXR side output read back from disk (half-float AOVs, temp files, delayed cancel); and a separate batch render (double cost, deep would not match the flat AOVs). Cost: about 15–22 days and 900–1,400 fork lines across moonray, mcrt_messages, mcrt_computation and hdMoonray; mcrt_messages and mcrt_computation need forking. Multi-machine merge (`progmcrt_merge`) gets no deep. Decided by the user on 2026-11-16, from M255 - Delegate Output Spikes' costing (see that milestone's Decisions).
