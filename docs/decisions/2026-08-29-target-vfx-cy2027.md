# Target the VFX Reference Platform — and get ahead of CY2027, not CY2026

2026-08-29. CY2026 (finalized, effective now) pins `gcc 14.2` + `glibc 2.28` —
Rocky Linux 8. The `glibc 2.34` figure is from the **CY2027 draft** (published
Apr 2026, not yet final), which moves the platform to EL9/Rocky Linux 9.

Decision: build on Rocky Linux 9 now rather than migrate glibc twice —
accepting the risk that the draft could still shift before it's final in Jan
2027. Fallback: swapping to `aswf/ci-baseqt:2026` (Rocky 8/glibc 2.28,
CY2026-final) is a one-line tag change in CI, not a rebuild of custom
infrastructure, if the draft direction changes.
