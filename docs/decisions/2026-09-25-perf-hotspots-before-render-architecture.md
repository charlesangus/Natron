# Fix the algorithmic hotspots before any render-architecture change

2026-09-25. A side session asked whether Natron needs a scanline, tiled or top-down render rework to handle thousands of nodes. It benchmarked synthetic chain/wide/comp graphs at 32×32 and HD with `tools/bench/` at `b0e212d5a`; results are in `build/bench/`. The answer was no, not first. Superlinear engine algorithms dominate at scale: the per-frame upstream walk for frame-varying checks (O(N²) per frame), the TLS copy for every holder on every multithreaded call, O(N²)-per-node auto-naming, an O(N) render-args setup on every connect, and an exponential cycle check on diamond DAGs. At HD, occupancy (1.0–1.7 of 4 cores) and host single-threaded copies matter more than tiling. The sequence is therefore M62 (hotspots, plus the bench committed as a regression harness) → M63 (task-graph scheduler, stub) → M64 (tiles, stub). M63 and M64 stay stubs until the previous milestone's re-benchmark justifies them. User decision (same day): this work runs after the channel/layer rework (M61, M37, M50, M60) and ahead of infra/housekeeping.
