# Required CI checks: Format, Lint CI, Code rules, Build and test

2026-11-09 (user). Branch protection on `main` requires four checks: `Format` (clang-format on changed C/C++ lines), `Lint CI` (actionlint, shellcheck, Slang/glslang pin check), `Code rules` (Serialization layering, kernel fma check), and `Build and test` (debug and release builds, every ctest leg, the cross-scheduler smoke comparison). They replace `format`, `lint-ci` and `build-and-test`. `lint-ci` had grown to enforce codebase rules its name didn't describe, so those rules moved to their own job. The gate workflows are renamed `Lint` and `Build and test`, and jobs use sentence-case `<Verb> · <what>` names. The rule from 2026-08-31-required-checks-name-real-jobs still holds: every required check names a real job, and renaming a job means updating protection in the same change. Lands with M377 - Readable CI Names.
